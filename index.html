Who We Are

LEAD COMPANY is a Canadian-based company operating in both the construction and supermarket retail industries. We are committed to delivering quality services and products while maintaining a strong focus on customer satisfaction.

Our strength lies in our diverse, international workforce. With team members from over 15 countries, we bring global perspectives and expertise to every project we undertake.
